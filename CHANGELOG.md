# Changelog

What changed in each release, in plain words.

## 0.1.0

The first public version. It works on the phones and computers we could test, but it is new: if
something goes wrong, please [open an issue](https://github.com/JannikHerter/beamcam/issues).

- Use your Android phone (Android 9 or newer) as a webcam on Linux and Windows 11, over your Wi-Fi.
- Click Connect on the computer and tap Allow on the phone. After that, opening both apps is enough.
- Front and back camera. Turn the phone sideways for a wide picture.
- Private: the video stays on your Wi-Fi and is encrypted. No account, no cloud.
- English and German.
